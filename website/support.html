Frequently asked questions

Is my data backed up?

Your journal entries, settings, and progress are stored only on your device. There is no cloud backup. We recommend keeping your device backed up (e.g. via Google One or a local backup) to avoid losing your data if you switch phones.

I switched phones — can I transfer my data?

Data is stored locally and does not transfer automatically between devices. Use Android's built-in app-data backup (Google One backup) when setting up a new phone to restore Daily Rituals data if available.

How do I reset my data?

Go to You → Settings → Reset app data inside the app, or uninstall and reinstall the app. This permanently deletes all local data.

I was charged but don't have Plus access

Open the app, go to You → Manage subscription → Restore purchases. If that doesn't resolve it, email us with your Google Play order number and we'll help investigate.

How do I cancel my Plus subscription?

Cancel through the Google Play Store → Subscriptions (not inside the app). You'll keep Plus access until the end of your current billing period.

I found a bug

Please email admin@destructaphoenix.dev with a description of what happened, your Android version, and steps to reproduce. Screenshots are very helpful.
