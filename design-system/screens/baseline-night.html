Baseline — night

The app as it actually is today, night mode. Real captures, not mockups.

Design from these, not from a description. This is the single biggest lever on whether output reads as the next version of Daily Rituals rather than a generic wellness app. Match the density, the card rhythm and the amount of breathing room you see here.
This is the dark theme — true AMOLED black, and the only one. Note what carries the depth here: there are no shadows in night mode. Surface contrast, a 1px c.border hairline and the card sheen do all of it.
Captured through npm run shots (Maestro + adb) against the storeShots dev scenario — a 210-day streak, "Sam", 2,400 embers — with the status bar in demo mode (12:00, full battery). The fixture is deliberately a heavy account: designs must survive big numbers.
