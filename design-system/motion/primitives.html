Motion primitives

Every export of src/motion.js, as a runnable approximation — so the house feel can be seen rather than inferred from a table. Press Replay on any card.

These are CSS approximations, not the implementation. The real primitives are Reanimated worklets. Use this page to judge feel and to pick the right primitive by name; never copy the CSS. In particular the spring is a bezier stand-in for a real physical spring (friction 5 / tension 80 — the pop lifted from Celebration.js, which is the house motion this vocabulary generalises).
