Bottom nav

The 4-tab bar plus the centre write FAB — src/RitualsApp.js. There is no navigation library.

The FAB overlaps the bar by 26dp and has a 6dp border in c.cream, so it punches a hole through the bar rather than sitting on it. That cut-out is the strongest single piece of brand in the chrome — keep it.
The bar background is c.navBg, which is deliberately translucent (92% white in day, 88% black in night) so content shows through as it scrolls under. Tab order is fixed: Today · Insights · Write · Reflections · You.
