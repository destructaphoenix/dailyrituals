Elevation

Depth comes from t.shadow(elevation), which is platform-split at source.

Android — the shipped platform — gets a single elevation number, not an offset/blur/spread. You cannot specify a shadow the way CSS does. Name t.shadow(8) and let it resolve; the preview below approximates it with the iOS geometry so the weight is visible, but the Android column is what actually renders.
The shadow is tinted, not black: c.shadowColor is a palette-derived warm tone in day mode and pure black in night. In night mode, depth comes mostly from surface contrast and hairline c.border — not from shadow.
