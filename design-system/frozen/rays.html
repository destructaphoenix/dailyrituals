The rays

The two hero backdrops — one per mode — rendered from the real components in src/art.js, at t=0.

FROZEN — reference only. This pair is the app's whole signature: the same 24-spoke fan, once for day and once for night. Compose around them. Never redraw, restyle, recolor, or re-time them. Designs may position them, size them, and animate their container (opacity, translate, scale) — nothing inside.
These are not mockups or redraws: each PNG is rasterised from the shipped component with the shipped default palette, so what you see is what renders. Both are captured at rotation 0 — RayFan and NightRays turn once per 60s in the app.
Nothing else is frozen. Any other artwork you find in the app is ordinary decoration, not brand: a design is free to replace it. If it is not on this card, it is not the signature.
