The empty band

Earlier takes moved content. That was wrong: nothing may sit in that band, because a Plus user has footage running there. So the band has to be filled by the layer that is already allowed to occupy it — the art. Sunrise, below, is art-only, and leaves the shipped text layout, the page header and every dp of type untouched. On the video ground it is not drawn at all: the sky was never the problem.

The band does not read empty because it has no content. It reads empty because the rays are at their faintest exactly where there is most of them to see: the convergence is a point of maximum density rendered at a flat 50% with no light around it. Fill the band with light — move the art's focal up into it and put a bloom behind the convergence — and 0 → 191 becomes the brightest part of the card without a single new element.

Why light and not content

the constraint, restated as a brief

The band's only legal occupant is the ground

On the video shell that band is footage. Anything drawn over it needs a scrim, and a scrim on a sky is the sky thrown away — which is also why a caption cannot live there. On the classic shell the same band is the rays. So the band is a ground in both shells, and the only fix available is to make the classic ground as worth looking at as the video one. That is a finite, achievable brief, and it is the one the card has never been given.

What "empty" actually is here

Rays are densest at the focal and sparsest at the tips. Since the card was pinned to 336 the focal sits at its centre, so the dense half of the art is behind the numeral and the band above it gets the thin ends — 24 near-parallel hairlines at 50%, with nothing behind them. The band is not missing an object. It is missing the part of the art that makes the art read.

Both treatments stay inside the rules

RayFan and NightRays are not redrawn: same 24 spokes, same 2dp stroke, same c.accent, same 60s rotation. Sunrise changes only focal and reach — the two props the brief already declares ordinary — and adds a radial bloom behind the convergence. It does not touch the height, the numeral or the meta row.

The numeral stays where it is

Card-y 127 → 252, centred, on HERO_FOCAL. Take two moved it and that was the wrong trade: the centred numeral is the asset. In A the art's focal and the numeral's anchor stop being the same number — the art gets its own focal at 96 and the numeral keeps the card's centre — which is the relationship the rays were composed for before the card was pinned.

Porting notes

for the build chat

What does not change

  • HERO_HEIGHT 336, HERO_BOX, HERO_FOCAL, both shells, the numeral block and its marginTop, the spacer, the meta row, ProgressBar — all untouched.
  • The page header above the card — date, EmberPill, mode toggle, greeting at 27/32 — stays exactly where it is. No user data, no text of any kind, enters the card.
  • The video shells: Sunrise changes nothing in them — it is classic-ground only.
  • RayFan / NightRays internals: 24 spokes, 2dp, c.accent, round caps, 60s. The candle row stays in the week strip.
  • Carried over from take two, and still open, and independent of this proposal: the video shells' own contrast fix — one 168dp bottom scrim (c.scrim → transparent, 62% dark skies / 58% c.plusWhite pale skies) and an ink set chosen from a new per-sky tone: 'pale' | 'dark' manifest field, because white type on a pale sky measures ~2:1 and c.accentDeep measures ~1.5:1. The two video mocks above are drawn with it. Ship it or don't, but decide it separately: nothing in A or B depends on it.

A · Sunrise — what changes

  • Art focal decouples from the numeral. New constant HERO_ART_FOCAL = 96, passed as focal; HERO_FOCAL keeps positioning the numeral. Two names because they are now two ideas.
  • Reach grows to clear the far corners from the higher focal: Math.hypot((w − 40) / 2, HERO_HEIGHT − HERO_ART_FOCAL) × 1.08 — 316 at 372dp, against 255 today. The tips stay outside the card at every rotation.
  • New radial fade on the fan (inside RayFan's wrapper, not its geometry): opaque to 26% of the radius, 55% at 52%, 12% at 78%, 0 at the rim. This is what keeps a 632dp fan from greying the lower card, and it is the one addition to the art component.
  • New Bloom behind the fan: a radial gradient, 210dp radius, centred on HERO_ART_FOCAL, c.accent at 26% → 10% → transparent at 72% (day) and 20% → 7% (night). Below streak 7 the two stops scale with the ray opacity ramp; at streak 0 it is not rendered.
  • HERO_ART_FOCAL is a constant at every streak, 0 included — only the fan's opacity and the bloom ramp. The streak-0 card above is focal 96 with the bloom absent, which is why its convergence still sits in the band.
